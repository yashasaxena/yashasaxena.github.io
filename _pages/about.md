Hi, my name is Yasha, and I am a fifth-year PhD candidate in Biomedical Engineering. My research combines 3D computer vision with in vitro experimental platforms to investigate how microarchitecture influences cellular behavior in hydrogel-based tissue-engineering materials.

Through my academic and professional experiences, I have developed into a well-rounded engineer with the skills needed to address complex clinical challenges. In the future, I hope to contribute to medical device research addressing critical healthcare needs while mentoring and educating the next generation of biomedical engineers.
