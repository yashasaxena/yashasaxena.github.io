My philosophy of engineering education has been shaped by both my academic experiences and the practical challenges I encountered during my five years in industry. I aim to equip students with the skills needed to address real-world biomedical engineering problems with project-based learning, while using the academic environment to cultivate curiosity, encourage thoughtful investigation, and provide a strong theoretical foundation.

As a PhD candidate in Biomedical Engineering, I have had the opportunity to teach and mentor undergraduates through engineering and science courses and projects. The following sections use the UK Professional Standards Framework (PSF) to describe my teaching approach and experiences.

A1. Designing and Planning Activities
A2. Teaching and Supporting Learners
A3. Assessing and Giving Feedback
A4. Mentoring and Guiding Learners
A5. Engaging in Professional Development
